## 🎯 What Makes NotebookLM Unique

**NotebookLM** is designed from the ground up for **source-grounded truth**:
- It searches **only** the documents you upload.
- Every single claim is backed by a clickable **inline citation badge** (`[1]`, `[2]`).
- If a fact is not in your sources, NotebookLM explicitly tells you rather than inventing an answer.

## 💡 Pro Tips for NotebookLM Forensic Research
1. **Targeted Follow-Up Inquiries**: After listening to an Audio Overview podcast, ask specific clarifying questions in the chat bar (e.g., *"What was the exact batch code and ISO standard discussed in the audio?"*).
2. **Selective Source Grounding**: In the left sidebar, uncheck all sources except the one you want to query to isolate specific laboratory certifications.
3. **Verify Citations**: Always click the small numbered citation badges to highlight the exact paragraph in your source text.

# 📚 Quick Reference Card: Gemini Notebook (NotebookLM)
### Mastering Grounded AI & Studio Tools for Fashion & Retail

---

## 🎯 Why NotebookLM Matters for Luxury Fashion & Manufacturing

Generic AI tools can hallucinate facts, invent certifications, or mix up optical standards. **NotebookLM** is designed from the ground up for **source-grounded truth**:
- It searches **only** the documents you upload.
- Every single claim is backed by a clickable **inline citation**.
- If a fact is not in your sources, NotebookLM admits it rather than inventing an answer.

## 🛠️ Core Capabilities Matrix

| Feature | How to Use | Best E-Commerce / Fashion Application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Multi-Source Ingestion** | Upload PDFs, Google Docs, Copied Text, URLs, or Slides. | Ingesting trend lookbooks, supplier audits, lab tests, and returns data into one brain. |
| **Inline Citations** | Click on numbered badges `[1]`, `[2]` in responses. | Auditing sustainability claims (ISO 14855) and UV safety regulations (EN ISO 12312-1). |

| **Curated Notes** | Click **"Save to Note"** on any response. | Compiling key takeaways that can be re-queried or exported directly into Google Docs. |

## 💡 Pro Tips for E-Commerce Teams
1. **Combine Disparate Formats**: You can upload a 30-page sustainability PDF alongside a short customer review spreadsheet and a runway press release. NotebookLM effortlessly bridges qualitative and quantitative data.
2. **Audio Overview Customization**: When generating an Audio Overview, you can guide the hosts on what to focus on (e.g., *"Focus on the Italian craftsmanship and the Asian-fit bridge ergonomics"*).
3. **Source Switching**: You can selectively check or uncheck individual sources in the left sidebar to isolate specific documents during research.

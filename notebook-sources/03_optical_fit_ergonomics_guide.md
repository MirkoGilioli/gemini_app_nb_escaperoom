## 1. Anatomy of Optical Sizing: The Boxing System
Eyewear measurements are expressed globally using the international standardized "boxing system" notation:
`[A Lens Width mm] - [DBL Distance Between Lenses / Bridge mm] - [Temple Length mm]`
*(Example: `54-18-145`)*

- **Lens Width (52–56 mm)**: Horizontal width of the lens at its widest point.
- **Distance Between Lenses / Bridge (16–21 mm)**: The horizontal gap over the nasal crest.
- **Temple Length (140–150 mm)**: Length of the arm from the hinge pin to the distal tip.

## 2. Standard Fit vs. Universal Low-Bridge Fit (Asian Fit)
A common cause of e-commerce returns in high-end eyewear is bridge mismatch. When a frame does not align with the wearer's nasal anatomy, two critical failure modes occur:
1. **Nasal Ridge Pinching & Pressure Points**: If the bridge is too narrow or lacks adjustable pads, the full weight of the frame concentrates on delicate nasal cartilage.
2. **Cheekbone Resting & Pantoscopic Tilt Error**: Wearers with higher cheekbones and lower nasal bridges experience the lower frame rim touching their cheeks, causing fogging, makeup transfer, and frame displacement during facial expressions.

### Technical Differences:
| Feature | Standard European Fit | Universal / Low-Bridge Fit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nose Pad Arms** | Fixed 3.2mm titanium stems | Extended 7.2mm gooseneck adjustable arms |

## 3. Customer Care & Retail Fitting Protocols
- **Medical Boundary**: Retail staff and digital customer service representatives are **strictly prohibited** from offering clinical or dermatological diagnoses for facial irritation. Redness, tension headaches, or skin reactions must always prompt a recommendation to consult an eye care professional (optometrist or ophthalmologist).
- **Proactive Resolution**: If a customer reports bridge pinch or fit discomfort, provide a complimentary appointment for specialized frame adjustment (angling pad arms, loosening temple spread) or offer an exchange for our **Universal Low-Bridge Fit** variant using the internal VIP care voucher: **`BRIDGEFIT26`**.

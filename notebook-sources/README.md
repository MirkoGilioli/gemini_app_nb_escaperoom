## 🧐 What is Gemini Notebook (NotebookLM)?

**NotebookLM** is Google's personalized AI research assistant powered by Gemini. Unlike generic chatbots that scour the open web or rely on general training data (which can lead to hallucinations), NotebookLM is **strictly grounded in the sources you upload**.

When you ask a question in NotebookLM:
- Every factual claim is anchored directly to your source text.
- Inline citation badges (e.g., `[1]`, `[2]`) show you the exact passage and page the answer came from.
- It features **NotebookLM Studio**, an integrated creative suite that turns your sources into:
  - **Audio Overviews**: A viral, two-host AI podcast conversation diving deep into your material.
  - **Briefing Docs**: Executive one-pagers summarizing core business initiatives.
  - **Study Guides & FAQs**: Structured learning material with review questions.
  - **Timelines**: Chronological breakdowns of audits, events, or production cycles.

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Setup Instructions for Participants

### 1. Open NotebookLM
Visit **[notebooklm.google.com](https://notebooklm.google.com)** and sign in with your Google Workspace or Google account.

### 2. Create Your Escape Room Notebook
Click **"+ New Notebook"** and name it:
```
Maison Solaris - Runway Launch Intelligence
```

### 3. Add Sources
You will find 4 realistic luxury eyewear dossiers in this folder:
- [`01_mido_eyewear_trend_report.md`](file:///Users/mirko.gilioli/gemini_app_notebook_escaperoom/notebook-sources/01_mido_eyewear_trend_report.md): Milan trend analysis, runway silhouettes, lens tint specifications.
- [`02_bio_acetate_material_audit.md`](file:///Users/mirko.gilioli/gemini_app_notebook_escaperoom/notebook-sources/02_bio_acetate_material_audit.md): Laboratory certifications, Mazzucchelli M49 bio-acetate tests, ISO 12312-1 UV ratings.
- [`03_optical_fit_ergonomics_guide.md`](file:///Users/mirko.gilioli/gemini_app_notebook_escaperoom/notebook-sources/03_optical_fit_ergonomics_guide.md): Bridge dimensions, pantoscopic tilt, Asian/Universal fit ergonomics.
- [`04_regional_preorder_sentiment.md`](file:///Users/mirko.gilioli/gemini_app_notebook_escaperoom/notebook-sources/04_regional_preorder_sentiment.md): Pre-orders, return rates, customer sentiment for Milan, NY, Tokyo.

> **How to upload to NotebookLM**:
> - Option A: Click **Add Source** -> **Copied Text** (paste the contents of each markdown file).
> - Option B: Upload as files (or import from Google Docs if you saved them to Drive).

### 4. Explore the Studio Panel
On the right-hand side of your NotebookLM screen, discover the **Studio** tools:
- **Audio Overview (Deep Dive)**: Click "Generate" to synthesize an engaging audio podcast discussing the collection!
- **Briefing Doc**: Click to create a comprehensive executive brief with one click.
- **FAQ / Study Guide**: Instantly generate customer care and retail training FAQs.

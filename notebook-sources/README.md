## 🧐 What is Gemini Notebook (NotebookLM)?

**NotebookLM** is Google's personalized AI research assistant powered by Gemini. Unlike generic chatbots, NotebookLM is **strictly grounded in the sources you upload**:
- Every factual claim is anchored directly to your source text with clickable **inline citation badges** (`[1]`, `[2]`).
- It features **NotebookLM Studio**, a comprehensive multimedia generative suite that turns your sources into:
  1. **Audio Overview (Podcast)**: A viral, two-host AI podcast conversation diving deep into your material.
  2. **Slide Decks & Presentations**: Automatically translates your research into structured presentation slide decks (Detailed Decks or Presenter Slides with speaker notes) with export to Google Slides.
  3. **Video Overviews**: Transforms your uploaded sources into narrated, AI-generated slideshow videos with visual elements, quotes, and diagrams.
  4. **Briefing Docs, FAQs & Timelines**: Structured one-pagers and chronologies for executive alignment.

## 3. Optical Tint Chemistry & Lens Standards

### Daylight Runway Specifications:
Fashion shows conducted under high-output daylight stage floodlights require strict luminous transmittance controls.
- **Sun Lens Rating**: All sunglasses featured on the Milan runway must comply strictly with **Category 3 (CAT3)** filter classification under international optical standard **EN ISO 12312-1**.
- **Luminous Transmittance Range**: Category 3 lenses allow precisely **8% to 18% visible light transmission (VLT)**, offering full UV400 absorption (100% UVA/UVB blocking up to 400nm) while maintaining natural color differentiation.
- **Tints**: Polarized smoky quartz gradient, warm amber flash, and deep midnight olive. Category 4 lenses (less than 8% VLT) are strictly barred from the runway and e-commerce general driving collections due to road safety prohibitions.

## 1. Mazzucchelli M49 Bio-Based Cellulose Acetate Certification
Maison Solaris has standardized all non-metal frame components on **Mazzucchelli M49** (Certified Batch Code: **DELTA-49**), a patented sustainable acetate formulation developed in Castiglione Olona, Italy.

### Key Laboratory Findings:
- **Bioplastic Formulation**: M49 replaces traditional phthalate-based chemical plasticizers with a proprietary vegetable-origin plasticizer derived from renewable wood pulp and cotton linters.
- **Biodegradability Standard**: Certified 100% biodegradable and compostable under standard **ISO 14855** (aerobic biodegradation under controlled composting conditions). It achieves greater than 90% organic disintegration within 115 days (2,760 continuous incubation hours) without leaving toxic residues or microplastics.
- **Batch Verification**: Batch **DELTA-49** has passed full spectroscopic purity analysis.
- **Biocompatibility & Hypoallergenic Testing**: Meets standard **ISO 10993-5** (biological evaluation of medical devices). Free of diethyl phthalate (DEP). Completely non-cytotoxic, ensuring no contact dermatitis or epidermal irritation during prolonged cutaneous wear.

## 2. Beta-Titanium Metallurgy (Grade-5 Ti-6Al-4V)
Wire and navigator frames produced in the Belluno artisan cluster utilize aerospace-grade cold-rolled Beta-Titanium.
- **Tensile Strength**: 950 MPa with superior shape memory recovery (spring hinge durability exceeding 50,000 continuous opening cycles).
- **Yield Strength**: 880 MPa.
- **Metallurgical Calibration Code**: The engineering certification token is **950TI**.
- **Nickel Migration Compliance**: Passes European REACH Regulation (EC No 1907/2006) and **EN 16128** with a certified zero-nickel release (<0.01 μg/cm²/week), safeguarding sensitive skin against contact eczema.
- **Total Weight**: Average raw frame weight is 12.8 grams before glazing.

## 3. Optical Sun Lens Transmission & UV Radiation Safety

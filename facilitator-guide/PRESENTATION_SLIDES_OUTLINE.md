## 🚀 Part 1: Kickoff Presentation (15 Minutes)

### Slide 1: Title & Atmospheric Hook
- **Visual**: Silhouette of a luxury titanium aviator frame gleaming in runway spotlights.
- **Header**: MAISON SOLARIS EYEWEAR: THE MIDNIGHT RUNWAY LOCKDOWN
- **Subheader**: An AI-Powered E-Commerce Escape Room with Gemini App Gems & Gemini Notebook (NotebookLM)
- **Facilitator Script**:
  > *"Welcome team. In exactly 60 minutes, Maison Solaris debuts its flagship A/W collection at Milan Fashion Week. 10 minutes ago, a critical security protocol froze our global e-commerce systems, corrupted the catalog database, triggered a viral influencer PR crisis, and drew scrutiny from European regulatory inspectors. Only mastery of Gemini App Gems and Gemini Notebook will reverse the breach before the spotlights turn on."*

### Slide 2: The Two Core AI Technologies
- **Visual**: Split slide comparing Gemini App (Custom Gems) vs. Gemini Notebook (NotebookLM).
- **Left Column: Google Gemini App (Custom Gems)**:
  - Custom AI personas with persistent system instructions.
  - Native tools enabled: **Nano Banana (Image Generation & Editing)**.
  - Internal **PDF Knowledge Bases** uploaded directly into the Gem.
  - Strict negative constraints & brand voice protection.
- **Right Column: Gemini Notebook (NotebookLM)**:
  - Strictly source-grounded intelligence with clickable inline citations.
  - **NotebookLM Studio**: Two-host **Audio Overview (Podcasts)**.
  - Automated **Slide Decks & Presentations** generation.
  - Automated **Video Overviews (Narrated AI Presentations)**.

### Slide 3: Squad Rules of Engagement
- **Squad Roles**: Merchandising Lead, Prompt Architect (Gems), Compliance Analyst (NotebookLM), PR Director.
- **Mission Console**: Open `index.html` on the squad laptop.

## 🏁 Part 2: Post-Mission Executive Debrief (15 Minutes)

### Slide 4: Chamber 01 Lessons — Optical Taxonomy & Nano Banana
- **Discussion**: Why is standard boxing notation (`54-18-145`) vital for e-commerce filtering?
- **Takeaway**: Uploading PDF knowledge into Gems grounds technical specs that generic models hallucinate, while native tools like **Nano Banana** enable instant visual merchandising asset generation.

### Slide 5: Chamber 02 Lessons — Audio Podcasts & Grounded Citations
- **Discussion**: How did generating the Audio Overview podcast reveal details you might have missed scanning 30 pages of text?
- **Takeaway**: NotebookLM's two-host podcast format synthesizes complex audits into digestible insights, and clickable citations ensure 100% legal compliance.

### Slide 6: Chamber 03 Lessons — Negative Constraints & Brand Safety
- **Discussion**: What happened when Chloe demanded headache medication?
- **Takeaway**: LLMs naturally want to be helpful and often attempt medical diagnoses. Rigorous negative constraints prevent severe legal liability while maintaining luxury empathy.

### Slide 7: Chamber 04 Lessons — Slides & Video Generation in NotebookLM
- **Discussion**: How does generating Slide Decks and Video Overviews directly in NotebookLM accelerate cross-functional alignment?
- **Takeaway**: Transforming grounded source research directly into executive slide decks and narrated videos eliminates hours of manual deck building before major product launches.

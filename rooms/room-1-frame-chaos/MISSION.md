# 🚪 Chamber 01: The Scrambled Optical Catalog
### Objective: Master Gemini App Gems with Default Tools (Nano Banana) & Internal PDF Knowledge

---

## 🚨 The Emergency
At T-minus 55 minutes, the supplier factory in Belluno transmitted raw manufacturing specs for the flagship runway frames, but an automated script corrupted our e-commerce database. The raw text is jumbled with factory jargon, chaotic millimeter measurements, and missing imagery.

The digital storefront requires three critical deliverables before the product can go live:
1. Standardized optical boxing notation: `[Lens] - [Bridge] - [Temple]`.
2. An evocative, high-converting luxury editorial PDP description.
3. An editorial visual concept generated using **Nano Banana (Gemini Image Generation)**.
4. The **Optical Calibration Override Code** derived from the company's confidential engineering blueprints.

---

## 🎯 Your Mission

1. **Deploy a Custom Gem in Gemini App with Tools & Knowledge**:
   - Go to [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com) -> **Gem Manager** -> **+ New Gem**.
   - Copy the System Instructions from [`gem-library/01_eyewear_catalog_gem.md`](file:///Users/mirko.gilioli/gemini_app_notebook_escaperoom/gem-library/01_eyewear_catalog_gem.md).
   - **Enable Default Tool**: Toggle ON **Image Generation (Nano Banana)**.
   - **Upload Knowledge**: Click **"Add files"** and upload `gem-library/knowledge-pdfs/Maison_Solaris_Technical_Blueprints.pdf`.
   - Save your Gem as `Maison Solaris Catalog Merchandiser`.

2. **Process the Raw Technical Specs**:
   - Open [`rooms/room-1-frame-chaos/raw_factory_specs.txt`](file:///Users/mirko.gilioli/gemini_app_notebook_escaperoom/rooms/room-1-frame-chaos/raw_factory_specs.txt).
   - Feed the spec for **Prototype #881: The Solstice Aviator** into your Gem.

3. **Generate Image & Extract the Hard-Mode Calibration Cipher**:
   - Prompt the Gem to render the visual product concept with Nano Banana.
   - Interrogate the Gem to extract the **Optical Calibration Code** based on Section 3 of the uploaded Blueprints PDF:
     `TITAN-[LENS_WIDTH]-[BRIDGE_WIDTH]-[TENSILE_STRENGTH]`
   - Verify the values in [`rooms/room-1-frame-chaos/student_worksheet.md`](file:///Users/mirko.gilioli/gemini_app_notebook_escaperoom/rooms/room-1-frame-chaos/student_worksheet.md).
   - Enter the resulting cipher into the Mission Control Console (`index.html`) to unlock Chamber 02!

# 🚪 Chamber 01: The Scrambled Optical Catalog
### Objective: Master Gemini App Gems for Structured Optical Taxonomy & Luxury Copy

---

## 🚨 The Emergency
At T-minus 55 minutes, the supplier factory in Belluno transmitted raw manufacturing specs for the flagship runway frames, but an automated script corrupted our e-commerce database. The raw text is jumbled with factory jargon, chaotic millimeter measurements, and broken markdown.

Without a standardized Product Detail Page (PDP) description and precise optical taxonomy, the global digital e-boutique cannot render the product page!

2. **Process the Raw Technical Specs**:
   - Open [`rooms/room-1-frame-chaos/raw_factory_specs.txt`](file:///Users/mirko.gilioli/gemini_app_notebook_escaperoom/rooms/room-1-frame-chaos/raw_factory_specs.txt).
   - Paste the spec for **Prototype #881: The Solstice Aviator** into your newly created Gem.

3. **Extract the 5-Letter Security Override Key**:
   - Inspect the standardized `OPTICAL TAXONOMY BLOCK` generated by your Gem.
   - Follow the instructions in [`rooms/room-1-frame-chaos/student_worksheet.md`](file:///Users/mirko.gilioli/gemini_app_notebook_escaperoom/rooms/room-1-frame-chaos/student_worksheet.md) to extract the first letter of each verified attribute tag.
   - Enter the resulting 5-letter cipher into the Mission Control Console (`index.html`) to unlock Chamber 02!

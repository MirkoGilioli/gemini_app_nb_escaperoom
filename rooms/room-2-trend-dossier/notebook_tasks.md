# 🔬 NotebookLM Forensic Tasks: Chamber 02

Execute the following prompts inside your **Gemini Notebook (NotebookLM)** to crack the compliance lock.

---

### Task 1: Sun Lens Safety & Filter Classification
**Prompt to type in NotebookLM**:
> *"What is the mandatory international sun lens filter category specified for sunglasses featured on the Milan runway according to EN ISO 12312-1? What is the exact visible light transmission range?"*

**Look for in the answer & citations**:
- The filter category code (e.g., `CAT0`, `CAT1`, `CAT2`, `CAT3`, or `CAT4`).
- Confirm the 8% to 18% luminous transmission range.
- **Key 1 extracted**: `CAT3`

---

### Task 2: Biodegradable Material Certification
**Prompt to type in NotebookLM**:
> *"What is the exact proprietary product code of the Mazzucchelli bio-acetate certified under ISO 14855 for 100% biodegradability? What natural ingredients replace traditional chemical plasticizers?"*

**Look for in the answer & citations**:
- The exact alphanumeric trade code (e.g., M49).
- The renewable sources (wood pulp and cotton linters).
- **Key 2 extracted**: `M49`

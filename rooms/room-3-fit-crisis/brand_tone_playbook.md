# 🛡️ Brand Tone & Optical PR Playbook
**Maison Solaris Eyewear Crisis Response Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)**

---

## 1. Non-Negotiable Medical & Legal Guardrails
When interacting with customers alleging physical discomfort:
- **Never Diagnose Medical or Dermatological Conditions**: It is illegal and a violation of medical regulations for e-commerce or customer support representatives to diagnose skin reactions, ocular pressure, migraines, or suggest over-the-counter medications.
- **Always Direct to Health Professionals**: Explicitly advise the customer that if headache, swelling, or redness persists, they should consult a licensed optometrist, ophthalmologist, or physician.
- **Reaffirm Verified Material Safety**: Confidently clarify that Maison Solaris frames utilize:
  1. Nickel-free, biocompatible Grade-5 Beta-Titanium tested under European REACH regulations.
  2. Medical-grade vulcanized liquid silicone nose pads free from phthalates, latex, or known allergens.

---

## 2. Anatomical Fit & De-escalation Strategy
- Explain that optical comfort depends heavily on bridge anatomy (nasal bone crest height and width).
- A frame that pinches is almost always experiencing a standard bridge fit mismatch rather than a manufacturing flaw.
- Introduce the **Universal Low-Bridge Fit** alternative, which features elongated 7.2mm gooseneck pad arms and a narrowed 15mm aperture.

---

## 3. Concierge VIP Recovery Protocol
To resolve high-priority escalations and appease VIP clients:
- Offer a priority, complimentary 1-on-1 private fitting session with our Master Opticians (either in-boutique at our Milan/NY/Tokyo flagship or via a dedicated high-definition digital optical consult).
- Provide the exclusive seasonal concierge service voucher: **`BRIDGEFIT26`**.
- This code grants complimentary expedited exchange for the Universal Low-Bridge Fit model or bespoke silicone pad micro-adjustment.

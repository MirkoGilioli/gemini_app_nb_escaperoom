## 🧐 What is a Gemini Gem?

A **Gem** is a customized version of Google Gemini that you configure with specialized **System Instructions**, knowledge, tone, and operational boundaries. Once created, a Gem acts as a dedicated AI teammate for recurring, specialized workflows—eliminating the need to re-explain your brand tone, formatting rules, or technical constraints in every new chat.

In the luxury fashion and eyewear industry, Gems empower cross-functional teams to:
- Maintain consistent luxury brand voice across thousands of SKU descriptions.
- Enforce optical engineering taxonomy and measurements (`lens-bridge-temple`).
- Establish strict negative constraints to prevent unauthorized medical/optometric claims.
- Automate multi-criteria e-commerce inventory decisions.

---

## 🛠️ How to Create a Gem in Google Gemini App

1. Navigate to **[gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com)** in your web browser.
2. In the left navigation sidebar, click on **Gem Manager** (or click the diamond **Explore Gems** icon).
3. Click **"+ New Gem"**.
4. Configure your Gem:
   - **Name**: Give your Gem a recognizable title (e.g., `Maison Solaris Catalog Merchandiser`).
   - **Instructions (System Prompt)**: Copy and paste the comprehensive system prompt from this library.
   - **Tone & Persona**: Clearly define how the Gem speaks (e.g., *"Sophisticated, precise, luxury Italian heritage"*).
5. Click **"Save"**.
6. Your Gem is now permanently pinned to your Gemini sidebar, ready to process incoming data!

## 📐 Anatomy of an Industry-Grade System Instruction

Every Gem in this library is crafted according to the **C-R-E-A-T-E** framework:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ C - CONTEXT & HERITAGE                                │
│     Who is the brand? (Maison Solaris Eyewear, Milan)  │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ R - ROLE & PERSONA                                     │
│     What is the Gem's job? (Senior Optical Copywriter) │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ E - EXPECTED INPUT                                     │
│     What will the user provide? (Raw factory specs)    │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ A - ACTIONS & LOGIC                                    │
│     How does it transform the input step-by-step?     │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ T - TAXONOMY & OUTPUT SCHEMA                           │
│     Exact structure: Title, PDP narrative, Specs JSON  │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ E - EXCLUSIONS & GUARDRAILS (Negative Constraints)     │
│     What must the Gem NEVER do? (No medical diagnosis) │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Reusable Custom AI Personas with System Instructions, PDF Knowledge & Default Tools

## 🧐 What is a Gemini Gem?

A **Gem** is a customized version of Google Gemini that you configure with specialized **System Instructions**, **uploaded Knowledge files (PDFs, Docs)**, **default tools (like Nano Banana Image Generation)**, and operational boundaries. Once created, a Gem acts as a dedicated AI teammate for recurring, specialized workflows.

In this training course, you will learn how to:
1. **Enable Default Tools in Gems**: Harness native tools like **Nano Banana (Image Generation)** to produce high-resolution product photography concepts directly inside the chat.
2. **Upload PDF Knowledge Bases**: Ground your Gem in internal engineering blueprints (`Maison_Solaris_Technical_Blueprints.pdf`) and brand safety standard operating procedures (`Maison_Solaris_Brand_Safety_SOP.pdf`).
3. **Enforce Strict Guardrails**: Instruct Gems to never provide medical/optometric diagnoses while delivering empathetic, high-touch luxury customer care.

---

## 🛠️ How to Create & Configure a Gem in Google Gemini App

1. Navigate to **[gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com)** in your web browser.
2. In the left navigation sidebar, click on **Gem Manager** (or click the diamond **Explore Gems** icon).
3. Click **"+ New Gem"**.
4. Configure your Gem:
   - **Name**: Give your Gem a recognizable title (e.g., `Maison Solaris Catalog Merchandiser`).
   - **Instructions (System Prompt)**: Copy and paste the system prompt from this library.
   - **Tools**: Ensure **Image Generation (Nano Banana)** is toggled ON.
   - **Knowledge**: Click **"Add files"** and upload the designated `.pdf` document from `gem-library/knowledge-pdfs/`.
5. Click **"Save"**.
6. Your Gem is now permanently pinned to your Gemini sidebar with embedded PDF knowledge and image rendering capabilities!
